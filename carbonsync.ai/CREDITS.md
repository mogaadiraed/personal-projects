The original project was carried out under the industrial supervision of Mr. Wael Gouader (YAP-T) and the academic supervision of Mr. Abdelbacet Mhamdi (Institute of Technological Studies of Bizerte), with support from the YAP-T Environment, Health and Safety team. ## Original Technologies & Methods

The original project used the following methods. This remake builds on the same ideas:

- **Emissions forecasting:** Meta's Prophet, with a linear-regression fallback
- **Predictive maintenance:** scikit-learn RandomForestClassifier
- **AI assistant:** NLP chatbot using NLTK and scikit-learn (TF-IDF + cosine similarity)
- **Stack:** React frontend, Flask backend, Docker Compose deployment
